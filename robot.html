Robotic Traverse — Closed-Loop Wind-Wake Study

An end-to-end robotic traverse system designed to position a sensor rig across a wind turbine wake for experimental fluid measurements. The system closes the loop with OptiTrack motion capture feeding a PID controller running on a Raspberry Pi, driving DC motors, with LabVIEW handling data acquisition and coordination.

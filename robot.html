Robotic Traverse — Closed-Loop Wind-Wake Study

01 The problem

What need or assignment prompted this? What constraints did you have — course requirements, lab goals, timeline, budget?

This project began as part of a wind energy research lab studying wake behavior behind scaled wind turbine models. The lab needed a way to automatically position a sensor across the wake with sub-millimeter repeatability, which ruled out manual positioning.

02 My approach

How did you plan the solution? What design decisions did you make, and why did you choose this architecture over alternatives?

I designed a closed-loop system: OptiTrack motion capture tracks the sensor rig's real-time position, a PID controller running on a Raspberry Pi computes the correction, and DC motors drive the traverse. LabVIEW coordinates data acquisition so the system logs both position and sensor readings in sync.

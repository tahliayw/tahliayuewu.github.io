01 The problem

What was the assignment or goal? What constraints did you have — class requirements, materials available, time in the shop?

This engine was the final project for [class name], a manufacturing processes course requiring a fully hand-machined, functioning mechanism built from raw stock, with no purchased pre-made parts.

02 My approach

How did you plan the build? Which parts needed manual machining vs. CNC, and why?

I modeled every component in CAD first, then split fabrication by process: the flywheel and cylinder were turned on the lathe, the base plate was cut on the manual mill, and geometrically complex parts were programmed in Mastercam and cut on the CNC mill.

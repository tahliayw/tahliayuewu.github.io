Stirling Engine — Machined From Stock

A running Stirling engine machined by hand for a manufacturing processes class, built from raw stock using a lathe, manual 3- and 2.5-axis milling, a vertical bandsaw, CNC machining in Mastercam, and laser-cut components, finished with a hand polish.
